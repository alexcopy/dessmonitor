READ-ONLY ARCHITECTURE FINDINGS

The existing components provide the intended boundaries:

- seasonal_output_strategy.evaluate_seasonal_strategy() already gates forecast,
  approved limits, reserve age and bounded seasonal proposals;
- grid_output_interlock.evaluate_interlock() owns recovery, charger readback,
  failed-trial, restart, cooldown, reserve evidence and advisory eligibility;
- adaptive_output_policy remains the underlying telemetry/solar/demand policy;
- app.battery_intervals.calculate_battery_intervals() rejects missing, invalid,
  conflicting, cached, duplicate and over-gap reported-current intervals;
- EnergyInputs and WinterContext are caller-supplied evidence containers, not
  proof that the caller independently verified the device or battery.

The corrective implementation must remain a composition/explanation layer. It
must not become a second controller, duplicate interval arithmetic, modify the
seasonal evaluator, weaken the Task 0076 interlock, or add runtime wiring.

CURRENT PROTOTYPE BEHAVIOR AND DEFECTS

1. The prototype has independent_reserve_alternative logic that can return
   BATTERY_USE_ELIGIBLE when the supplied interlock is
   SOLAR_RETURN_CANDIDATE with a HOLD decision. A BMS/shunt provenance string is
   treated as enough to bypass the missing post-recovery Invert evidence. This
   violates the Task 0076 authority boundary.

2. reserve_verified checks battery_energy_verified, usable_battery_wh and a
   source-name allowlist, but does not require a valid, current, non-future
   reserve observation. A caller-supplied boolean and source string are not
   independent measurement verification.

3. forecast_confident checks confidence and a non-null provenance string but does
   not itself enforce forecast age, future timestamps or validity-window semantics.
   A three-day-old forecast can therefore be described as VERIFIED by the winter
   evidence layer even though the existing seasonal evaluator would hold.

4. _age() clamps negative ages to zero. Future-dated observations can therefore
   look current in WinterEvidence. Missing or invalid timestamps are not a valid
   basis for current eligibility.

5. reported_interval_status == COMPLETE is presented as VERIFIED interval
   evidence even when the caller supplies only that status without the underlying
   observations. COMPLETE from app.battery_intervals describes contiguous
   EASUN-reported-channel integration; it does not prove total battery current,
   BMS reserve, capacity or source identity.

6. Diagnostics are present, but the evidence contract does not consistently keep
   source provenance, measurement validity and freshness as independent fields.
   A stale or future value must remain visible as stale/future/unverified rather
   than being promoted by a confidence label.

7. The prototype contains useful shadow states, research voltage labels and
   deadlock reasons, but the corrective plan must preserve those diagnostics while
   making them unable to authorize a physical or advisory battery return unless
   Task 0076 itself has granted advisory eligibility.

CORRECTIVE DESIGN

1. INTERLOCK AUTHORITY INVARIANT

The only path to WinterState.BATTERY_USE_ELIGIBLE is:

  interlock is present
  AND interlock.state == InterlockState.BATTERY_RETURN_ELIGIBLE
  AND interlock.decision == InterlockAction.ADVISE
  AND interlock.advisory_only is True
  AND interlock.command_issued is False
  AND interlock.application_authorized_trial is False
  AND the seasonal strategy and independent evidence gates pass.

An interlock HOLD, BLOCK, missing decision, GRID_OUTPUT_HOLD,
SOLAR_RETURN_CANDIDATE, GRID_RECOVERY_COMPLETE, FAULT_HOLD or CHURN_LOCKOUT
must never produce BATTERY_USE_ELIGIBLE, regardless of BMS/shunt evidence.
The implementation must remove or neutralize the current bypass path.

Independent BMS/shunt evidence may be reported as
INDEPENDENT_RESERVE_AVAILABLE_INTERLOCK_PENDING and may reduce a diagnostic
missing-requirement list, but it cannot override Task 0076. A post-recovery
deadlock therefore remains SOLAR_RETURN_CANDIDATE or RECOVERY_OR_FAULT_HOLD,
with an explicit deadlock reason, until the interlock independently grants
eligibility. A supervised trial remains a future operator-approved process, not
an implicit authorization.

2. SEPARATE EVIDENCE DIMENSIONS

Refactor the winter evidence representation so each item preserves, independently:

- source_provenance: for example EASUN_REPORTED, WEATHER_FORECAST, BMS, SHUNT,
  BMS_SHUNT, APP_BATTERY_INTERVALS or UNKNOWN;
- measurement_validity: VALID, INCOMPLETE, INVALID or UNVERIFIED;
- freshness: CURRENT, STALE, FUTURE or UNKNOWN;
- observed_at and signed age_seconds, retaining negative age as FUTURE rather
  than clamping it to zero;
- confidence/explanation derived from those fields, never used to override them.

The evidence item must retain a valid diagnostic value and its failure status even
when the decision is blocked. Missing timestamps remain UNKNOWN and cannot produce
CURRENT or VERIFIED evidence.

3. FORECAST AND MEASUREMENT FRESHNESS

Use existing approved configuration rather than new hardware thresholds:

- forecast freshness and validity must use
  OutputPolicyBaseline.forecast_max_age_seconds plus the existing forecast issue
  and valid-through checks already enforced by seasonal_output_strategy;
- measured telemetry freshness must use
  OutputPolicyBaseline.telemetry_max_age_seconds;
- reserve freshness must use the supplied EnergyInputs.max_energy_age_seconds
  only after it is finite, positive, and the reserve observation timestamp is
  aware, not future-dated and no older than that bound.

A three-day-old forecast must be labelled STALE/UNVERIFIED and must not be
  forecast-current evidence. Future-dated forecast, PV, or reserve observations
  must be labelled FUTURE/UNVERIFIED and block current eligibility. No new
  battery-voltage, BMS, capacity, current or seasonal threshold may be invented.

4. RESERVE AND INTERVAL VALIDATION

The caller must provide validated underlying reserve observations, not only
reserve_verified=True, reserve_provenance='BMS_SHUNT', or an interval status.
The implementation must require, at minimum, an independently identified BMS or
shunt evidence source, a valid non-future observation timestamp, an approved age
bound, finite usable reserve, and a measurement-validity result that is not
INCOMPLETE, INVALID or UNVERIFIED.

When EASUN-reported throughput is supplied, the adapter must use
app.battery_intervals.calculate_battery_intervals() on the underlying ordered
observations. Missing current/voltage, invalid voltage, conflicting currents,
cached repeats, duplicate timestamps and gaps remain barriers. A caller-supplied
COMPLETE string without the underlying validated result must remain
UNVERIFIED. Even a genuine COMPLETE EASUN interval result is a DC reported-channel
proxy, not independently verified total battery reserve or capacity.

The winter layer may expose interval coverage, rejected barriers and proxy
throughput as diagnostics. It must not convert those diagnostics into BMS/shunt
verification or use PV/output power to invent battery energy.

5. DEADLOCK REPORTING

When Utility First/recovery history leaves no comparable loaded Invert evidence,
preserve a stable diagnostic reason such as
POST_RECOVERY_EVIDENCE_DEADLOCK. The result must identify the missing alternative:
validated Task 0076 reserve evidence, independently validated BMS/shunt reserve,
or a separately supervised operator trial. The layer must not bypass Task 0076,
start a trial, alter output priority, alter charger priority, or claim that
continuous Utility First operation proves battery reserve.

6. RESEARCH VOLTAGE LABELS

Keep 25.5 V, 26.5 V, 27.0 V, 27.5 V and 25.0–25.4 V as explicitly labelled
research observations only. They are not guards, commands, SOC estimates,
capacity estimates or eligibility criteria. A fast voltage rise remains a
reported observation and cannot prove solar support, low capacity or usable
reserve.

REQUIRED REGRESSION TESTS

Add deterministic tests proving:

1. SOLAR_RETURN_CANDIDATE plus InterlockAction.HOLD remains not eligible even
   with independently identified BMS/shunt reserve. The result preserves the
   reserve evidence and reports interlock pending/deadlock diagnostics.

2. InterlockAction.BLOCK, FAULT_HOLD, CHURN_LOCKOUT, GRID_RECOVERY_COMPLETE and
   a missing interlock all remain not eligible, regardless of reserve evidence.

3. A three-day-old forecast is STALE/UNVERIFIED using the existing approved age
   bound, not VERIFIED/CURRENT.

4. Stale and future-dated reserve observations fail current eligibility while
   retaining their measured value, provenance and freshness failure in diagnostics.

5. Caller-supplied COMPLETE without underlying interval observations is
   UNVERIFIED. A validated interval result remains an EASUN-channel proxy and
   cannot create independent BMS/shunt reserve provenance.

6. Missing, naive, malformed and future timestamps fail closed without a negative
   age being converted to zero/current.

7. Valid diagnostics remain present when eligibility is blocked: output demand,
   PV, voltage, reported currents, interval barriers, reserve source and missing
   requirements are not discarded.

8. Emergency recovery, nighttime charging, charger thresholds, scheduled
   recovery, solar takeover, Task 0076 lockouts and restart behavior remain
   unchanged through the composed decision layer.

9. No output-source, charger-source, DESS, Tuya, relay or production command is
   issued or represented as authorized. Use static import checks and command spies
   where the existing tests support them.

10. Research voltage observations at 25.5/26.5/27.0/27.5 V and 25.0–25.4 V
    never independently change an ineligible interlock into an eligible result.
